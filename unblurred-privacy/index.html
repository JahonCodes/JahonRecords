Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Overview

Unblurred is a productivity and focus app that helps users manage tasks, projects, and goals through focus sessions and voice notes. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app.

Data Collection

Unblurred collects the following types of data:

Audio Data

  • What: Voice recordings from microphone
  • Purpose: Voice-to-text transcription for brain dump entries
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only
  • Sharing: Not shared with any third parties

User Content

  • What: Tasks, projects, goals, notes, and focus session data
  • Purpose: Core app functionality and data persistence
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only
  • Sharing: Not shared with any third parties

App Usage Data

  • What: App preferences, settings, and focus session timers
  • Purpose: App functionality and user experience
  • Storage: Stored locally on your device only
  • Sharing: Not shared with any third parties

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • Data is not used for advertising or analytics
  • Data is not sold to any third parties

Permissions

Unblurred requests the following permissions:

Microphone Permission

  • Purpose: To record voice notes for brain dump entries
  • Usage: Only when you actively choose to record voice notes
  • Control: You can revoke this permission in your device settings at any time

Your Rights

  • Delete Data: You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke microphone permission in device settings
  • Data Portability: All data remains on your device and is not transmitted elsewhere
  • No Account Required: The app works without creating an account

Third-Party Services

Unblurred does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Unblurred does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app and on this page.
