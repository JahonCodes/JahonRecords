Troubleshooting

Common issues and quick solutions

Voice Input Issues

Check microphone permissions in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone

• Ensure you're in a quiet environment

• Try speaking clearly and at normal volume

• Restart the app if issues persist

App Crashes

Force-close and reopen the app, or restart your device

• Make sure you have the latest app version

• Check that your device meets iOS 13.0+ requirement

• Free up storage space if needed

Timer Problems

Make sure you've selected a valid duration and try restarting the timer

• Check that the app isn't in background too long

• Try selecting a different time duration

• Restart the focus session

Data Not Saving

Check device storage space and try force-closing the app

• Ensure you have enough storage space

• Check that you're entering valid text

• Try force-closing and reopening the app
